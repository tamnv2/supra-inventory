## PickList incident 12:50–12:52 VN

Four distinct PDA requests created in Firestore and received by VPS PRIMARY succeeded at network/ACK level yet resulted in REQUEST_EXPIRED without any WMS confirm. At receipt, authenticated Firestore created-age was 230–393 milliseconds, while Agent measured client-age 23,432–23,716 milliseconds, above the 12,000ms D160 mutation fence. Same VPS confirmed three other Picker jobs within these two minutes. Four similar failures occurred for the affected PDA at 12:22–12:23.
